\section*{Python Operator Exercises (Week 3)}

For this week, we will focus on various types of operators in Python (refer to pages 23-30 of the lecture notes).  

This exercise will help you practice:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Arithmetic operators} for calculations
    \item \textbf{Comparison operators} to compare values
    \item \textbf{Logical operators} to combine conditions
    \item \textbf{Membership operators} to check strings
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Exercise}

\textbf{Shopping Bill Calculator} \hfill \textbf{[100 marks]}

\subsection*{Problem Scenario}

You are shopping at a local store and want to create a simple program to calculate your bill and determine if you qualify for discounts.

The store has the following rules:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Students get a 10\% discount
    \item Purchases over E500 get a 5\% discount
    \item Local customers (from Eswatini) get free delivery
    \item International customers pay E50 delivery fee
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Your Task}

Write a Python program that:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Asks the customer for:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Their name
        \item Whether they are a student (yes/no)
        \item Their country
        \item Price of first item
        \item Price of second item
        \item Price of third item
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item Calculates:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Total cost (sum of three items)
        \item Student discount amount (10\% if applicable)
        \item Bulk discount amount (5\% if total over E500)
        \item Delivery fee (E0 for Eswatini, E50 for others)
        \item Final amount to pay
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item Displays:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Customer name
        \item Subtotal (before discounts)
        \item Whether customer is a student
        \item Whether customer qualifies for bulk discount
        \item Total discount amount
        \item Delivery fee
        \item Final bill amount
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Requirements}

Your program must use:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Arithmetic operators} (+, -, *, /) to calculate totals and discounts
    \item \textbf{Comparison operators} (>, >=, ==) to check discount eligibility
    \item \textbf{Logical operators} (and, or) to combine conditions
    \item \textbf{Membership operators} (in, not in) to check if "Eswatini" is in the country name
\end{itemize}
